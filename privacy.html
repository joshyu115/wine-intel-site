Privacy Policy — Wine Intel

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Wine Intel is a personal tool used by a single user (its operator).

What it accesses

With the operator's authorization, Wine Intel uses the Google Drive API (drive.file and drive.readonly scopes). It can read files in the operator's Google Drive, in read-only mode, to retrieve screenshots specified by the operator. It creates and edits only its own archive and export files, and it never modifies or deletes the operator's existing files.

What it stores

Exports and backups of the operator's own working notes, stored in the operator's own Google Drive folder and on the operator's own server.

What it does not do

It does not collect data from any other person, does not sell or share data, does not use data for advertising, and does not track users.
